Cookie policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This website does not use cookies, first-party or third-party. The only thing it can store in your browser is the language you pick, and only if you pick it.

What is stored

Data this website can store in your browser
NameTypePurposeWhen it is createdHow long it lasts
sakura.lang Browser local storage (localStorage), not a cookie Remember the language you chose (es or en) so you are not sent to the other one Only when you press the ES/EN switch Until you clear this site's data in your browser

That value is never sent to a server: local storage stays in your browser and, unlike a cookie, does not travel with requests.

Versions of this website before the date above also stored sakura.lang.auto on the first visit. It is no longer created, and the website deletes it if it finds it.

Automatic language choice

If you open the Spanish home page and your browser is set to another language, the website takes you to the English version. To do that it reads the language your browser announces, at that moment, without storing it. As soon as you choose with the switch, your choice is respected.

Why there is no consent banner

The rules that require asking permission before storing something in the browser — such as the European ePrivacy Directive — exempt what is strictly necessary for a service the person has explicitly asked for. Remembering the language you have just chosen with a button falls within that exemption, according to Opinion 04/2012 of the European data protection authorities. There is nothing else to store, so there is nothing to consent to.

Third parties

The website loads nothing from other servers. When you press a download button or a link to GitHub, you leave this website for github.com, which has its own cookie policy.

How to delete it

From your browser settings, in the site data or cookies section, by clearing the data for exotara.github.io.
